Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Lookback is a personal memory journal. Your data belongs to you.

What we collect

Lookback stores the memories you create — photos, videos, voice notes, text, emoji, and links — along with your email address and display name. This data is stored securely in Supabase and is only accessible to you.

AI processing

When you generate a monthly lookback, your memories are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI to produce a personalised story. This data is sent to Anthropic solely to generate your lookback and is not used to train AI models.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with third parties, except as described above.
  • We do not use your memories for advertising.

Data storage

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure. All data is scoped to your account and protected by row-level security policies.

Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen in the app. Deleting your account permanently removes all your data.
